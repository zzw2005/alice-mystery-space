# Vendored: pixelfx

The `core/` and `fx/` directories in this folder are copied from
[patch-gg/pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx) (commit d74d8c2 era,
244 effects), licensed under the MIT License — see `LICENSE.pixelfx.txt`.

Only two effects are bundled: `fx/pickups/collect-burst.js` and
`fx/ui/confetti.js` (plus their per-category `_lib.js`). Individual effect
files carry their own additional credits (Sprite Fusion / Hugo Duprez, MIT)
in their headers; those headers are preserved verbatim.

`pixel-layer.js` in this folder is Alice's own player code, not part of pixelfx.
